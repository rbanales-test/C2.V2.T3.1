Noise Sculpting Simulator

Objective: Transform a random, unpitched signal (White Noise) into useful musical elements using envelopes and filters, overcoming a sound design challenge.

Instructions:

  1. Click Start Simulator. The audio engine will generate raw White Noise.
  2. Press and hold the large trigger button to hear the sound.
  3. Complete the 5 sequential missions to turn the static hiss into a percussive hit, and finally into a howling wind soundscape.
  4. The visualizer will show the frequency spectrum (Spectrum Analyzer).
