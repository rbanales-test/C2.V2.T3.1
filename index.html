Noise Sculpting Simulator

⚠️ Volume Warning / Cuidado con el Volumen

This simulator produces broadband white noise across the entire audible spectrum. Please adjust your headphones or audio output to a moderate level before starting.

Objective: Transform a random, unpitched signal (White Noise) into useful musical elements using envelopes and filters.

Instructions & Accessibility:

  1. Click Start Simulator to initialize the engine (integrated safety limiter active).
  2. Press and hold Spacebar or the on-screen button to trigger and hold the noise.
  3. Complete the 5 sequential missions of ascending complexity.
  4. The first 4 missions are guided to teach you the modules; the final mission is an unguided challenge.

Sculpting Noise

White noise contains equal energy at all frequencies. Watch the frequency spectrum change as you carve it.

Amplifier Envelope
Shapes the volume over time.
Low-Pass Filter (Sculptor)
Reduces high frequencies to darken the tone.
Mission 1/5: The Raw Static
Press and hold the trigger. Listen to the raw, unshaped white noise and watch the flat spectrum.
